# BCM Starter Kit

A single-file, offline Business Continuity Management workbook. No
installation, no account, no cloud — open `bcm-starter-kit.html` in a
browser and start working. Process records, business impact analysis,
critical resource tracking, emergency operations planning, resilience
checks, a measures catalog, versioning, and PDF reporting, all client-side.

Apache License 2.0. Originally developed by Marco Riemer
([riemer-consulting.de](https://www.riemer-consulting.de)).

**Status:** functionally complete. Bug fixes, documentation, and
accessibility contributions welcome — see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
